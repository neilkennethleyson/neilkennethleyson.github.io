02 — Reproduction

Steps to Reproduce

Log in using a valid customer-service account.
Navigate to the Customer Profile page.
Open an existing customer record.
Change the customer's status from Active to Inactive.
Click Save.
Refresh the page and review the customer's status.
03 — Analysis

Expected vs Actual

Expected Result

After saving the profile, the customer's status should be updated to Inactive and remain Inactive after the page is refreshed.

Actual Result

The interface initially displays the updated status, but after refreshing the page the customer's status returns to Active.

04 — Investigation

Investigation & Impact

Initial Observation

The UI appears to accept the change successfully, suggesting that the initial client-side operation completed without displaying an error.

Persistence Check

Refreshing the page reveals that the updated value was not persisted correctly.

Business Impact

Users may rely on an incorrect customer status, potentially causing incorrect downstream processing or reporting.

Recommended Investigation

Review the save request, API response, persistence layer, and subsequent profile retrieval to determine where the state is lost.

05 — QA Thinking

Why This Matters

A good defect report should reduce back-and-forth.

Clear reproduction steps, precise expected and actual results, environment details, frequency, and business impact give developers and stakeholders enough context to understand the problem quickly.


My approach is to investigate beyond the visible symptom whenever possible. I consider the complete workflow and ask where the data, state, or expected behavior may have broken down.
